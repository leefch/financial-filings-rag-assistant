Item 1. Business
Globex Industries is a diversified specialty chemicals producer serving the
coatings, adhesives, and water-treatment markets. The company operates twelve
manufacturing plants across North America and Europe and sells through both
direct sales and distributors.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Net sales for fiscal 2024 were $3.05 billion, essentially flat versus $3.07
billion in fiscal 2023, as pricing gains were offset by lower volumes in the
coatings market. Adjusted EBITDA was $512 million, a margin of 16.8%. The
company reduced net debt by $210 million during the year and ended the period
with a net-debt-to-EBITDA ratio of 2.1x, down from 2.6x a year earlier.

The company declared dividends of $1.28 per share during fiscal 2024 and
repurchased $150 million of common stock under its existing authorization.

Item 1A. Risk Factors
Globex's raw material costs, particularly petrochemical feedstocks, are volatile
and can compress margins when the company is unable to pass through cost
increases. Environmental regulation is a significant and increasing compliance
cost, and the company is responsible for remediation at several legacy sites.
Demand in the coatings segment is tied to construction and automotive
production, both of which are cyclical.
