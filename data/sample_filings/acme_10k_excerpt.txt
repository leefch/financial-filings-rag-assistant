Item 1. Business
ACME Corporation designs and manufactures industrial automation equipment for
warehouse and logistics customers. The company operates two segments: Robotics
and Aftermarket Services. Founded in 1998, ACME is headquartered in Columbus,
Ohio and employs approximately 4,200 people as of the fiscal year end.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $1.82 billion, an increase of 11% from
$1.64 billion in fiscal 2023. The Robotics segment contributed $1.24 billion of
total revenue, while Aftermarket Services contributed $580 million. Gross margin
improved to 38.5% from 36.1% in the prior year, driven by higher-margin service
contracts and improved manufacturing efficiency. Operating income was $246
million, and net income was $181 million, or $3.42 per diluted share.

Research and development expense was $138 million in fiscal 2024, representing
approximately 7.6% of revenue, consistent with the company's stated target of
investing 7% to 8% of revenue in R&D.

Item 1A. Risk Factors
The company's results depend heavily on capital spending by logistics and
e-commerce customers, which is cyclical and sensitive to interest rates. A
prolonged slowdown in warehouse construction would reduce demand for the
Robotics segment. ACME also depends on a limited number of suppliers for
precision servo motors; disruption at these suppliers could delay shipments.
Approximately 22% of revenue is generated outside the United States, exposing
the company to currency fluctuation and trade policy risk.
